Common questions

The app does not find my adapter.
Check that the adapter is plugged in, the ignition is on, and Bluetooth permission is granted to Doktor.

Where is my data?
Diagnostic data stays on your device.

Something is broken.
Write to contact@kolobovdjp.org with your device model, OS version, app version and what happened. Please do not send personal data, passwords or pairing files.
