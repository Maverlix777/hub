Privacy Policy: Doktor

Last updated: 2026-10-25

Operator: KOLOBOVDJP LLC. Contact: contact@kolobovdjp.org.

What the app does

Doktor is an OBD-II diagnostic app. It connects over Bluetooth Low Energy to an OBD adapter in your vehicle and shows diagnostic trouble codes and live readings.

Data that stays on your device

Diagnostic data read from your vehicle, trouble codes, readings and app settings stay on your device. We do not receive them. The app has no accounts and no sign-in, and it does not use your location.

Requests to NHTSA

To decode a VIN (vehicle identification number), the app can send the first 11 characters of the VIN to the public NHTSA vPIC decoder service (a U.S. government service). To show recalls and complaints, it can send the vehicle make, model and model year to the NHTSA API. These requests contain no name, email or account. NHTSA processes them under its own terms; connection details such as IP address are visible to that service.

Advertising

The app (iOS and Android) shows ads through Google AdMob. On iOS the ads are non-personalized, the app does not track users and does not show the App Tracking Transparency prompt. AdMob may collect device identifiers, coarse location from the IP address, and usage and diagnostic data under Google’s policies. Where required, a consent form is shown. We do not receive this data and we do not sell personal data.

Ask the Doctor (AI)

The Ask the Doctor feature can work in three ways. (1) Offline, with an on-device model: this needs an optional download of about 1 GB from a model host (Hugging Face), and the download request reveals your IP address to that host. (2) With your own API key for a third-party AI service (Anthropic or xAI): your question and the vehicle data you include are sent to that service only after you agree, and the service processes them under its own terms. (3) With built-in rules, which need no network. AI advice can be wrong and does not replace a mechanic.

Data we collect ourselves

None.

Bluetooth

Bluetooth permission is used only to talk to your OBD adapter.

Children

The app is a vehicle tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone.
